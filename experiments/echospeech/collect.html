Silent commands

Mouth each word silently, with lip movements similar to or slightly larger than speaking out loud. No voice, no whisper. Keep your head still between words.

Each word stays up to 3 seconds. Press space as soon as you have finished, x to repeat the word, p to pause. After the session, take the glasses off and put them back on before starting the next one.
